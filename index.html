Juan David Garcia-Gonzalez

Hola, soy Juan.

I am a PhD candidate at Bocconi University and an Adjunct Lecturer of Economics at Universidad de la Sabana. I use quasi-experimental and experimental methods to study education and migration issues. I focus on the transition to higher education and student migration: what drives students to enter college, what drives them to move, and how these choices shape their education, labor and innovation outcomes.
